Privacy

What stays on your iPhone

The camera image and the 2000s look are processed on your iPhone. No photo, no face and no picture data is ever sent to us or to anyone else. There is no AI and no server.

Your photos

Each shot is saved to your Photos library with add-only access: Overflash cannot read your library. A copy is kept inside the app for playback; deleting the app deletes that copy. Photos you develop are picked by you, one at a time, with the system photo picker.

No account, no tracking

Overflash has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code that collects data.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Apple. We never see your name or your payment method.
