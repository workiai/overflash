Where are my photos?

Every shot is saved to your Photos library as soon as you take it, and a copy stays in the app (PLAY). If a shot shows "NOT IN PHOTOS", allow Overflash to add photos in Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos.

The viewfinder is black

Allow camera access in Settings › Overflash › Camera.

Restore Full Card

Open MENU › SETUP › RESTORE, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

Cancel the yearly plan

Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Overflash. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal; Full Card stays on until the end of the paid period.
